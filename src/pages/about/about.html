Why I built this

I love finding new music. I can lose a whole evening on Bandcamp: one album leads to a label, the label to an artist I'd never heard of, and somewhere around the fourth tab I find something I can't stop playing. It's my favorite hobby.

What I wanted was a place that always has something new for me. Music that's new to me, but makes sense with what I already love. Not what's trending, and not more of the same.

Streaming recommendations stopped doing that a while ago. On Bandcamp, the albums people buy and save point to each other, and your own collection and wishlist say a lot about your taste. This extension reads those and does the digging for you.

What you get

A fresh list every day: albums you don't own, from artists you don't have, close to what you actually listen to. Save what you love to your wishlist, hide what makes no sense, and the next list gets sharper.

The goal is simple: a list where you'd like every single album. Each album you hide gets you closer to that.

If you also love finding new music, I hope this saves you some digging and gives you something good to listen to today.

How it works

  • Each day, pick what to hear: My tags (explore new music by genre), Best matches (perfect matches with your collection) or Surprise me (go beyond your collection).
  • It starts from what you own and save, and finds albums that fans of those records love.
  • You only see what's new to you: nothing you own, nothing repeated.
  • Click the heart to wishlist an album, + to save songs to Liked Songs, ⊘ to hide what you don't want. Every choice sharpens the next list. The heart is the only thing that touches your Bandcamp account.
  • Listen right here, one song per album or full albums, even while your list is loading.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. The extension uses your existing Bandcamp session to read your collection and wishlist, and only talks to Bandcamp (bandcamp.com, and the servers that stream its audio). Nothing is sent to any other server, and your password is never seen.
