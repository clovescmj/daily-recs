Why I built this

I love finding new music. I can lose a whole evening on Bandcamp: one album leads to a label, the label to an artist I'd never heard of, and somewhere around the fourth tab I find something I can't stop playing. It's my favorite hobby.

What I wanted was a place that always has something new for me. Music that's new to me, but makes sense with what I already love. Not what's trending, and not more of the same.

Streaming recommendations stopped doing that a while ago. On Bandcamp, the albums people buy and save point to each other, and your own collection and wishlist say a lot about your taste. This extension reads those and does the digging for you.

What you get

A fresh list every day: albums you don't own, from artists you don't have, close to what you actually listen to. Save what you love to your wishlist, hide what makes no sense, and the next list gets sharper.

The goal is simple: a list where you'd like every single album. Each album you hide gets you closer to that.

If you also love finding new music, I hope this saves you some digging and gives you something good to listen to today.

How it works

  • Each day you choose what to hear: your tags, the best matches, or a surprise.
  • Starts from what you own and what you've saved on Bandcamp.
  • Finds albums that fans of those records love, and keeps the ones that fit your taste.
  • Shows only what's new to you: nothing you own, nothing repeated.
  • The heart adds an album to your Bandcamp wishlist, and it is the only button that changes your Bandcamp account.
  • The + adds songs to Liked Songs, a list that lives in your browser: on a card it adds the whole album, in the player bar only the song that is playing. What you like feeds the next lists, and you won't see those albums again.
  • The ⊘ means "don't show music like this": that album, its artist and the albums Bandcamp pairs with it stop coming. Inside a genre list it only hides them in lists of the same genres.
  • My tags: explore new music by genre.
  • Best matches: perfect matches with your collection.
  • Surprise me: go beyond your collection.

Listening

Play any album from its cover, or press play at the top to go through the list. The Playback button in the player bar chooses between one song per album (the track the artist highlights, album after album) and the full album. The queue shows the list, and Liked Songs shows what you saved.

While a list is being built, the loading box offers a song from your collection. It fades out and your list starts when it is ready.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. The extension uses your existing Bandcamp session to read your collection and wishlist, and only talks to Bandcamp (bandcamp.com, and the servers that stream its audio). Nothing is sent to any other server, and your password is never seen.
