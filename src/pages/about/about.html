Why I built this

I love finding new music. I can lose a whole evening on Bandcamp: one album leads to a label, the label to an artist I'd never heard of, and somewhere around the fourth tab I find something I can't stop playing. It's my favorite hobby.

What I wanted was a place that always has something new for me. Music I don't know, but that makes sense with what I already love. Not what's trending, and not more of the same.

Streaming recommendations stopped doing that a while ago. On Bandcamp, the albums people buy and save point to each other, and your own collection and wishlist say a lot about your taste. This extension reads those and does the digging for you.

What you get

A fresh list every day: albums you don't own, from artists you don't have, close to what you actually listen to. Like what you love, hide what you don't, and the next list gets sharper.

The goal is simple: a list where you'd like every single album. Each like and each dislike gets you closer to that.

If you also love finding new music, I hope this saves you some digging and gives you something good to listen to today.

How it works

  • Starts from what you own and what you've saved on Bandcamp.
  • Finds albums that fans of those records love, and keeps the ones that fit your taste.
  • Shows only what's new to you: nothing you own, nothing repeated.
  • Heart what you love, hide what you don't, and the next list gets sharper.
  • Surprise me goes a little further from the obvious.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. The extension uses your existing Bandcamp session to read your collection and wishlist, and only talks to bandcamp.com. Nothing is sent to any other server, and your password is never seen.
