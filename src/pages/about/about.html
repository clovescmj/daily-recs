Why I built this

I love finding new music. I can lose a whole evening on Bandcamp: one album leads to a label, the label to an artist I'd never heard of, and somewhere around the fourth tab I find something I can't stop playing. It's my favorite hobby.

What I wanted was a place that always has something new for me. Music that's new to me, but makes sense with what I already love. Not what's trending, and not more of the same.

Streaming recommendations stopped doing that a while ago. On Bandcamp, the albums people buy and save point to each other, and your own collection and wishlist say a lot about your taste. This extension reads those and does the digging for you.

What you get

A fresh list every day: albums you don't own, from artists you don't have, close to what you actually listen to. Save what you love to your wishlist, hide what makes no sense, and the next list gets sharper.

The goal is simple: a list where you'd like every single album. Each album you hide gets you closer to that.

If you also love finding new music, I hope this saves you some digging and gives you something good to listen to today.

How it works

  • Each day, pick what to hear: My tags (explore new music by genre), Best matches (perfect matches with your collection) or Surprise me (go beyond your collection).
  • It starts from what you own and save, and finds albums that fans of those records love.
  • You only see what's new to you: nothing you own, nothing repeated.
  • Click the heart to wishlist an album, + to save songs to Liked Songs, ⊘ to hide what you don't want. Every choice sharpens the next list. The heart is the only thing that touches your Bandcamp account.
  • Listen right here, with a Shuffle button for one song from each album, even while your first list is loading.

Privacy and the data it uses

Everything runs in your browser. The extension uses the Bandcamp session you are already signed in with, and only talks to Bandcamp (bandcamp.com, and the servers that stream its audio and covers). It never sees your password. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server, and there is no analytics or advertising.

What it reads

  • Your collection and wishlist (titles, artists and links), to know what you already have.
  • The "you may also like" section, genre tags and songs of album pages, to find new music. While the browser is open it reads a few of your albums' pages in the background, about every half hour, only to learn their genres.

What it keeps, and where

  • On this computer: a snapshot of your library, the day's lists, your genre profile, your Liked Songs, and what played today (so the player can come back after a reload).
  • In your Google account (Chrome sync): the albums you wishlist with the heart and the albums you hide, with a score for each source of recommendations. Only ids and codes, no titles or links. You control it with Chrome's sync settings.
  • Liked Songs never leave this browser.

What it changes on Bandcamp

  • Only your wishlist, and only when you click the heart. Hiding an album or liking a song never changes your Bandcamp account.
  • It does not run on Bandcamp's sign-in, settings, cart, checkout or purchases pages.

The full text is in the privacy policy.

Your data

Everything Daily Recs keeps is in this browser: a snapshot of your library, today's lists, Liked Songs and your taste. You can clear it here. Your Bandcamp account is not touched. On your other computers, clear it there too, or the taste synced through your Google account comes back.

This can't be undone.
