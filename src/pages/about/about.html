About Daily Recs

Version – · Developed by Clóves C. · Bandcamp · Instagram

Why I built this

I love finding new music. I can lose a whole evening on Bandcamp: one album leads to a label, the label to an artist I'd never heard of, and around the fourth tab I find something I can't stop playing. It's my favorite hobby.

I wanted a place that always has something new for me, close to what I already love. I don't want what's trending, and I don't want more of the same. Streaming recommendations stopped doing that for me a while ago.

On Bandcamp, the albums people buy and save point to each other: every album page lists what its fans also own. Daily Recs follows those links from your collection and wishlist and does the digging for you.

What you get

Every day you get a list of albums you don't own, from artists you don't have yet, picked from what fans of your own albums also own. If you also love finding new music, I hope it saves you some digging and gives you something good to play today.

The goal is a list where you'd like every album. Three buttons teach it your taste:

  • ⊘ Hide an album and it never comes back. Its artist and the albums Bandcamp pairs with it stop coming too.
  • Heart an album to add it to your Bandcamp wishlist, and + a song to add it to Liked Songs. Either way, the extension reads that album's recommendations right away, so similar music shows up in the next lists.

How it works

  • You pick one list a day. Best matches has the albums that the most fans of your albums also own. My tags has albums with the genres you choose. Surprise me goes deeper into the same ranking, so it's further from what you own, and never repeats Best matches.
  • Albums you own or have in your wishlist never show up, and neither does an album you were already shown.
  • The heart is the only button that changes your Bandcamp account. Hiding an album or adding a song to Liked Songs stays inside the extension.
  • You can listen right on the page. Full album plays each album in order. Shuffle plays one song from each album, in random order, and never the same song twice. While your first list loads, it plays a random song from your own collection.

Sync

In your Google account Checking…

The albums you hide, and a score for each source of recommendations, are saved in your Google account through Chrome sync, so your other computers get them. Chrome sync has to be on. Your wishlist hearts and your Liked Songs are not synced: they stay on this computer.

Privacy and the data it uses

Daily Recs runs in your browser and talks only to Bandcamp (bandcamp.com and the servers that serve its audio and covers). It uses the Bandcamp session you already have open, so it never sees your password. Nothing goes to the developer or to any other server. There are no analytics and no ads.

What it reads

  • Your collection and wishlist (titles, artists and links), to know what you already have.
  • The album pages of those albums and of the ones it finds: the "you may also like" list, the genre tags and the song list. While the browser is open it also reads a few of your albums' pages in the background, about every 30 minutes, only to learn their genres.

What it keeps, and where

  • On this computer: a copy of your library (titles, artists, links), the day's lists, your genre profile, the albums you added to your wishlist from here, your Liked Songs, and what played today with the position of the song, so the player picks up where it was after a reload.
  • In your Google account: the albums you hide and the score of each source, as ids and codes. No titles and no links. Chrome's sync settings control it.

What it changes on Bandcamp

  • Your wishlist, and only when you click the heart.
  • It doesn't load on Bandcamp's sign-in, settings, cart, checkout and purchases pages.

The full text is in the privacy policy.

Your data

Clear saved data removes everything listed above from this browser: the library copy, the lists, Liked Songs, the wishlist hearts and the hidden albums kept in your Google account. Your Bandcamp account and wishlist stay as they are. If you use Daily Recs on other computers, clear it there too, or they will send the hidden albums back to your Google account.

This can't be undone.
