1.5 · One song per card

  • Each card is one song. It shows the song, the artist and the album. The heart adds that song to your Bandcamp wishlist and the + adds it to Liked Songs.
  • No more Shuffle. The player plays the card's song and then the next card, in the order you see them. A song played from Liked Songs goes on with the Liked Songs.
  • The tag of the daily recs tab says "new list" until you pick the list of the day, instead of a number.
  • What you did not play can come back. Only the albums you played (a song for a few seconds) stay out of the next days' lists. Before, everything that was shown stayed out, played or not.
  • A tab left open past midnight shows a line at the bottom: "A new day has started", with a button to get the new list.
  • A line at the bottom of the page says when there is news in a version, with a button for this page. Another one offers to update when a new version is ready, and confirmations (a song added, a wishlist change) have an Undo.

1.4 · Better suggestions, and more of them

  • Stricter rules for what gets in. An album only enters a list if it shares a genre with your taste. Albums whose genres could not be read no longer fill the empty spots.
  • More variety. No genre takes more than about a fifth of a list, and two albums of the same genre are not played one after the other.
  • More albums. As soon as your list loads, 50 more are added in the background, and 50 more each time you get close to the end, up to 300 in a day.
  • Shuffle works like a radio. One song from each album, and an album does not come back the same day. When the loaded albums run out, it asks for more.
  • A list can now be shorter than 50 when few albums fit your taste. That is on purpose: it is better to see fewer albums than to see ones that do not belong.
  • Skipping a song changes nothing, so you can skip a style for today without teaching the extension that you do not like it.

1.3 · Your data, in plain sight

  • About says what the extension reads, what it keeps and where, and what it changes on Bandcamp.
  • A tab left open overnight shows a notice when a new day starts. "Get new recs" asks for today's list of the same kind, and nothing changes until you click it.
  • "Export liked songs" at the bottom of the Liked Songs list saves them as a CSV file (artist, song, album and link).
  • A "Clear saved data" button in About removes everything the extension saved in your browser and in your Google account.
  • The extension no longer loads on Bandcamp's sign-in, settings, cart, checkout and purchases pages.
  • The record of the albums you added to your wishlist from here follows your real wishlist on Bandcamp.
  • A new logo, and a small dot on the toolbar icon when a version with news arrives. Right-click the icon and choose "What's new" to see it.

1.2 · Shuffle, a song queue and a fresh look

  • A Shuffle button that plays one song from each album, in random order, and never the same song twice.
  • The queue lists songs one after another, like a streaming app, and opens on the song that is playing. After a reload it comes back with what played, the song and its position (for that day).
  • Liked Songs shows covers, a count and a footer. A song is saved the moment you click the +, and stays until you remove it. When you save one, its cover flies into the Liked Songs icon.
  • New icons, a tidier player bar, and a "new" tag on the daily recs tab in the style of Bandcamp's own tags.
  • The song from your collection keeps playing when your list is ready. Its button shows only while the first list loads.

1.0 · First stable version

  • A new daily recs tab on your Bandcamp profile with a fresh list every day: My tags, Best matches or Surprise me.
  • The heart adds an album to your wishlist, the + saves songs to Liked Songs, and the ⊘ hides music you don't want.
  • A player bar in Bandcamp's style, with a queue and your Liked Songs.
  • While your list is being built, you can play a random song from your own collection.
  • Cleaner genre suggestions: tags written as a list (like "punk #ebm #lofi") are split into separate genres, and places and typos are left out.
  • A look that follows Bandcamp's colors and type.
