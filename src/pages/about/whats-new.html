1.3 · Your data, in plain sight

  • About says what the extension reads, what it keeps and where, and what it changes on Bandcamp.
  • "Export liked songs" at the bottom of the Liked Songs list saves them as a CSV file (artist, song, album and link).
  • A "Clear saved data" button in About removes everything the extension saved in your browser and in your Google account.
  • The extension no longer loads on Bandcamp's sign-in, settings, cart, checkout and purchases pages.
  • The record of the albums you added to your wishlist from here follows your real wishlist on Bandcamp.
  • A new logo, and a small dot on the toolbar icon when a version with news arrives. Right-click the icon and choose "What's new" to see it.

1.2 · Shuffle, a song queue and a fresh look

  • A Shuffle button that plays one song from each album, in random order, and never the same song twice.
  • The queue lists songs one after another, like a streaming app, and opens on the song that is playing. After a reload it comes back with what played, the song and its position (for that day).
  • Liked Songs shows covers, a count and a footer. A song is saved the moment you click the +, and stays until you remove it. When you save one, its cover flies into the Liked Songs icon.
  • New icons, a tidier player bar, and a "new" tag on the daily recs tab in the style of Bandcamp's own tags.
  • The song from your collection keeps playing when your list is ready. Its button shows only while the first list loads.

1.0 · First stable version

  • A new daily recs tab on your Bandcamp profile with a fresh list every day: My tags, Best matches or Surprise me.
  • The heart adds an album to your wishlist, the + saves songs to Liked Songs, and the ⊘ hides music you don't want.
  • A player bar in Bandcamp's style, with a queue and your Liked Songs.
  • While your list is being built, you can play a random song from your own collection.
  • Cleaner genre suggestions: tags written as a list (like "punk #ebm #lofi") are split into separate genres, and places and typos are left out.
  • A look that follows Bandcamp's colors and type.
