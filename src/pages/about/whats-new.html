What's new

1.1.6 · A new loading button

  • The button that plays a song from your collection while the list loads has a new look. A long song name scrolls inside it.

1.1.5 · A text fix

  • Only the genre menu keeps the full stops added in 1.1.4. Other messages are back as they were.

1.1.4 · Small text fixes

  • The genre menu is wider, so its first line no longer breaks.
  • Sentences in the extension end with a full stop.

1.1.3 · New play mode icons

  • Shuffle and Full album have new icons, and the shuffle one is smaller.

1.1.2 · Matching icons

  • The play mode icons have the same size as the other icons on the right of the player bar.

1.1.1 · Two play modes

  • Play mode is now a pair of buttons: Full album (the default) and Shuffle. Hover them to see what each does.
  • "One song per album" was merged into Shuffle, which plays one song from each album in a random order.

1.1.0 · Play mode and Shuffle

  • "Playback" is now "Play mode", with a new Shuffle option: one song per album, in a random order.
  • A song never plays twice. When the list ends, the next round plays other songs from the same albums, until everything has played.
  • The queue follows the mode, and the previous button goes back to the song that played before.

1.0.9 · Music keeps playing

  • If a song from your collection is playing while your list loads, it keeps playing when the list is ready, and shows in the player bar.

1.0.8 · A fix

  • The + on an album card works again: it adds the album to Liked Songs and lights up.

1.0.7 · Better lists

  • The queue and Liked Songs now look alike, with covers, two-line rows, a count and a footer.
  • The queue groups albums as Played, Playing now and Up next.

1.0.6 · Liked Songs and cards

  • Saving one song from the player no longer marks the whole album on its card as added.

1.0.5 · A tidier player bar

  • The play button, the progress bar and the buttons of the player bar are aligned on the same centre as the cover.

1.0.4 · Liked Songs, safer

  • Your Liked Songs are saved the moment you click the +, and nothing but you can change them: not a search, not an update. They only go away if you uninstall the extension.

1.0.3 · A nicer Liked Songs

  • When you save a song, its cover flies into the Liked Songs icon, which jumps as if it kept it.

1.0.2 · Consistent wording

  • The same names everywhere: Liked Songs, Recommendations queue, and tags inside the tags menu.
  • Clearer messages when you add or remove an album, and one message when Bandcamp asks us to slow down.

1.0.1 · Small things

  • This page: a short note about each release, opened from About.

1.0 · First stable version

  • A new daily recs tab on your Bandcamp profile, with a fresh list every day: My tags, Best matches or Surprise me.
  • The heart adds an album to your wishlist, the + saves songs to Liked Songs, and the ⊘ hides music you don't want.
  • A player bar in Bandcamp's style: one song per album or full albums, a queue and your Liked Songs.
  • While your list is being built, you can play a random song from your own collection.
  • Cleaner genre suggestions: lists of hashtags are split into genres, and places and typos are left out.
  • A new look that follows Bandcamp's own colours and type, with smooth transitions.
