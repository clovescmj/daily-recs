1.2 · Shuffle, a song queue and a fresh look

  • A Shuffle button that plays random songs from your recommendations.
  • The queue lists the songs one after the other, like a streaming app, and opens on what is playing. Liked Songs has covers, a count and a footer.
  • Your Liked Songs are saved the moment you click the +, and only you can change them. When you save a song, its cover flies into the Liked Songs icon.
  • A new set of icons, and a tidier player bar.
  • A song from your collection keeps playing when your list is ready, and its button shows only while your first list loads.

1.0 · First stable version

  • A new daily recs tab on your Bandcamp profile, with a fresh list every day: My tags, Best matches or Surprise me.
  • The heart adds an album to your wishlist, the + saves songs to Liked Songs, and the ⊘ hides music you don't want.
  • A player bar in Bandcamp's style: one song per album or full albums, a queue and your Liked Songs.
  • While your list is being built, you can play a random song from your own collection.
  • Cleaner genre suggestions: lists of hashtags are split into genres, and places and typos are left out.
  • A new look that follows Bandcamp's own colours and type, with smooth transitions.
